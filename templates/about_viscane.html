⚙System Overview

Executive summary

From crop observation to confident action.

VISCANE is an edge-based artificial intelligence system that analyzes sugarcane images using RGB-HSV imaging for quality grading and maturity detection. It uses computer vision and machine learning to provide accessible assessments that support better harvest and farm decisions.

The platform combines image analysis with agronomic information so users can review results, estimate yield, receive recommendations, and maintain organized records in one connected experience.

🔍

The problem

Traditional grading can be subjective and slow, delaying feedback and making consistent crop evaluation difficult.

🎯

The purpose

Deliver accessible AI-powered quality grading, maturity detection, yield estimation, and agronomic guidance.

⚙System Workflow

VISCANE guides the user through a clear sequence from image capture to actionable farm guidance.

  3. 03

  4. 04
