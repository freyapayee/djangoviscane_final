About the platform

Smarter sugarcane decisions with VISCANE.

VISCANE is an edge-based artificial intelligence system for sugarcane quality grading. It helps farmers capture crop information, receive clear quality insights, and maintain organized farm records in one place.

🌿

Executive summary

Turning crop observations into useful guidance

VISCANE supports sugarcane farmers and agricultural decision-makers by combining image-based analysis with agronomic information. The system is designed to make quality assessment more accessible, consistent, and actionable—from submitting a scan to reviewing recommendations and historical records.

By bringing these tasks into one platform, VISCANE helps users understand crop conditions, plan next steps, and keep a dependable digital record of their farm activities.
